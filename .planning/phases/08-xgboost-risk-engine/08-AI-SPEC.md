## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Hybrid (Supervised Tabular Gradient Boosted Decision Trees + Rule-Based Statutory Safeguards)

**Description:**
A high-precision, two-stage fraud risk calibration and explainability sub-system for the PM-KISAN subsidy portal. It ingests the multi-dimensional outputs from the 9 existing anomaly engines (Identity, Land, Bank, Exclusion, Duplicate Parcel, Statistical Density, Temporal Spikes, Isolation Forest, and NetworkX Syndicate Graph) along with raw application claims, transforms them into a normalized 24-dimensional feature vector, and uses a calibrated XGBoost meta-classifier to predict non-linear fraud probability (0–100). Crucially, the system enforces a strict Boolean statutory override (locking risk to 100 for legal disqualifications under Rule 4 of PM-KISAN) and computes exact local feature attributions using TreeSHAP so scheme officers receive an auditable waterfall of the top drivers triggering an investigation.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. **Statutory Ineligibility Under-Scoring**: Assigning a non-critical risk score (e.g. < 75) to an applicant who meets statutory disqualification criteria (e.g., income tax payer, deceased individual, government pensioner, or invalid cadastral title) due to ML probability dilution.
2. **Adversarial Threshold Gaming**: Fraudulent syndicates exploiting linear decision tree splits by staying marginally below isolated thresholds (e.g., claiming 1.99 Ha instead of 2.01 Ha).
3. **Discriminatory False Positives on Vulnerable Farmers**: Wrongfully penalizing genuine smallholder and marginal farmers due to non-fraudulent phonetic naming variations between Devanagari revenue records and Romanized Aadhaar transliterations.
4. **Black-Box Unexplainability**: Presenting an ML fraud score to an administrative officer without legally grounded, feature-level attribution (violating natural justice principles under Indian administrative law).

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched for direct benefit transfer (DBT) agricultural subsidy schemes under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.

**Industry Vertical:** Public Sector / Agritech / Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Fraud Detection

**User Population:** Scheme Adjudication Officers (District Agriculture Officers, Tehsildars, Revenue Patwaris, and State Nodal Reviewers)

**Stakes Level:** High (Direct disbursement of public funds from the Consolidated Fund of India; erroneous denial deprives genuine subsistence farmers of crucial seasonal liquidity; wrongful disbursement invites CAG audits, vigilance probes, and recovery proceedings under General Financial Rules 2017).

**Output Consequence:** Advisory risk scoring and decision triage presented inside the Scheme Officer Review Console. The system strictly adheres to human-in-the-loop governance: the officer retains sole legal authority to `APPROVE`, `HOLD`, `REJECT`, or `REQUEST_DOCUMENTS`. The ML model never automatically authorizes payment or executes cancellations.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

| Dimension | Good (Expert Accepts) | Bad (Expert Flags) | Stakes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Statutory Rule Adherence** | Ineligible applicants (taxpayers, civil servants, dead persons) are immediately disqualified ($Risk = 100$) regardless of other clean features. | Ineligible applicant given moderate score ($Risk = 55$) because bank and land area appeared clean. | Critical (Statutory non-compliance; CAG audit liability) | PM-KISAN Operational Guidelines, Section 4 |
| **Syndicate Non-Linear Interception** | Intercepts multi-signal shell registrations (e.g. shared bank account + moderate fuzzy name discrepancy + pre-deadline burst). | Evaluates each discrepancy in isolation as "Low", missing the organized syndicate pattern. | High (Mass leakages in DBT tranche disbursements) | Anti-Fraud Task Force Reports |
| **Phonetic & Dialect Fairness** | Tolerates regional transliteration mismatches (e.g. "Ramji" vs "Ram Ji", "Laxmi" vs "Lakshmi") when land and biometric credentials match. | Flags honest rural cultivators as fraudsters over simple spelling variations. | High (Exclusion of genuine marginalized beneficiaries) | UIDAI Transliteration Standards |
| **Auditability & Explainability** | Provides exact SHAP feature contribution waterfalls citing why the application exceeded standard risk thresholds. | Opaque score without feature-level breakdown; officer cannot defend reasons during appellate review. | High (Article 14 Due Process & Natural Justice) | Central Administrative Tribunal Precedents |

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain
- **Transliteration Gap**: Discrepancies between English Roman script and State land record vernacular scripts (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu) artificially inflating fuzzy distance scores.
- **Village-Level Spatial Burst False Positives**: Legitimate collective registration drives conducted by village Common Service Centers (CSCs) triggering statistical temporal spike engines without fraudulent intent.
- **Mule Account Syndicates**: Professional agents using a single bank account or mobile number across multiple unrelated elderly/widowed beneficiaries.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context
- **PM-KISAN Scheme Operational Guidelines (Rule 4 Exclusion Clauses)**: Non-negotiable exclusion of constitutional post holders, serving/retired civil servants, professionals, and income tax payers.
- **Aadhaar Act 2016 & DPDP Act 2023**: Raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers must never enter the feature vector or model training set; only salted cryptographic hashes (`aadhaar_ref`) and topological graph connectivity metrics may be processed.
- **General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017**: Mandates audit logging, transparency, and defensible criteria for public subsidy allocations.

### Domain Expert Roles for Evaluation

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Scheme Vigilance Officer** | Validates that zero statutory exclusions are approved or assigned non-critical risk tiers. |
| **Lead Applied ML Engineer** | Audits ROC-AUC, Brier score calibration, TreeSHAP consistency, and inference latency benchmarks. |
| **Field Revenue Inspector (Patwari)** | Reviews high-risk syndicate edge cases to ensure feature splits reflect actual rural land tenure dynamics. |

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** `xgboost>=2.0.0` + `shap>=0.44.0` (with `scikit-learn>=1.4.0`)

**Version:** Pin `xgboost==2.0.3` and `shap==0.44.1`

**Rationale:**
1. **Unrivaled Tabular Performance**: Gradient-boosted decision trees consistently outperform deep learning (TabNet/MLP) on heterogeneous tabular datasets with numerical, ordinal, and binary flags.
2. **Sub-5ms C++ Inference Latency**: XGBoost's compiled native engine processes individual 24-dimensional feature vectors in < 3ms, allowing inline synchronous evaluation during HTTP request handling without introducing async worker queues.
3. **Exact TreeSHAP Attribution**: Native integration with Lundberg's TreeSHAP algorithm computes exact polynomial-time Shapley values, providing mathematically rigorous, additive feature attribution without sampling approximations.
4. **100% Offline Hackathon Resilience**: Runs completely self-contained in Python with local wheel binaries; zero external cloud API keys, network calls, or GPU dependencies required.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|---|---|
| **LightGBM** | Excellent speed, but Windows PowerShell wheel compilation and OpenMP library linkages frequently cause binary installation hurdles on heterogeneous evaluation environments. |
| **CatBoost** | Excellent categorical handling, but larger binary package size (~100MB) and slower CPU inference on small vector sizes compared to XGBoost. |
| **PyTorch / TabNet** | Overkill parameter footprint, requires extensive hyperparameter tuning, susceptible to overfitting on small/synthetic seed datasets, and lacks native exact TreeSHAP computation. |
| **Pure LLM Prompting** | Non-deterministic, expensive, high latency (2–6s), legal unreliability in mathematical scoring, and vulnerable to prompt injection. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** No. Model produces standard scikit-learn compatible estimator API and exports to portable JSON/UBJSON format.

### Core Imports
```python
import xgboost as xgb
import shap
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from typing import Dict, List, Tuple, Any
```

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
# 1. Feature Vectorization (24 features)
feature_vector = extract_feature_vector(application_details, engine_flags, db)
X = np.array([feature_vector], dtype=np.float32)

# 2. Hard Statutory Exclusion Safeguard
if check_statutory_exclusion_hit(engine_flags):
    ml_risk_score = 100
    is_override = True
    shap_drivers = [("STATUTORY_EXCLUSION_OVERRIDE", 1.0)]
else:
    # 3. Supervised Model Inference
    dmatrix = xgb.DMatrix(X, feature_names=FEATURE_NAMES)
    raw_prob = float(booster.predict(dmatrix)[0])
    ml_risk_score = int(round(raw_prob * 100))
    is_override = False

    # 4. TreeSHAP Attribution
    shap_values = explainer(X)
    top_indices = np.argsort(np.abs(shap_values.values[0]))[::-1][:3]
    shap_drivers = [
        (FEATURE_NAMES[idx], float(shap_values.values[0][idx]))
        for idx in top_indices
    ]
```

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---|---|---|
| `xgb.Booster` / `xgb.XGBClassifier` | Core gradient boosting tree estimator | Trained on multi-engine synthetic ground-truth and loaded for inference. |
| `xgb.DMatrix` | Optimized internal data layout for XGBoost | Low-overhead vector evaluation during live API scoring. |
| `shap.TreeExplainer` | Fast exact Shapley value explainer for tree ensembles | Calculates feature-level positive/negative contributions for every claim. |
| `TwoStageRiskScorer` | Architectural orchestrator | Evaluates Boolean statutory rules first, then applies ML calibration and SHAP. |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **Feature Name Mismatches**: Passing an unlabelled NumPy array to a booster trained with named features emits warnings or misaligns SHAP attributions. *Remedy: Always use explicitly typed `FEATURE_NAMES` arrays.*
2. **Missing Feature Imputation**: Zero vs NaN handling in tree split routing. *Remedy: Use consistent default fill values (`-1.0` for missing match ratios, `0` for counts).*
3. **SHAP Background Distribution Explosion**: Using thousands of background samples in `TreeExplainer` slows down explanation. *Remedy: Use `TreeExplainer(booster, feature_perturbation="tree_path_dependent")` which requires 0 background samples and runs in < 2ms.*

## 4. Implementation Guidance

### Model Configuration
- **Algorithm**: XGBoost Binary Logistic Classifier (`objective="binary:logistic"`, `eval_metric="logloss"`)
- **Hyperparameters**:
  - `max_depth`: 4 (avoids overfitting to idiosyncratic synthetic anomalies; captures 3-way interactions)
  - `learning_rate`: 0.08
  - `n_estimators`: 120
  - `subsample`: 0.85
  - `colsample_bytree`: 0.85
  - `scale_pos_weight`: 1.5 (balances high cost of fraudulent false negatives)
  - `random_state`: 42 (100% deterministic reproducibility)

### The 24-Dimensional Feature Vector Specification
The feature vector maps both primary application claims and secondary engine outputs:
1. `declared_land_area_ha` (float): Declared cultivable land size.
2. `applicant_age_years` (float): Derived from DOB.
3. `days_to_nearest_event` (float): Proximity to DBT calendar milestone.
4. `fuzzy_name_match_score` (float: 0–100): From Identity/Bhulekh engine.
5. `dob_match_flag` (binary: 0 or 1): Identity DOB consistency.
6. `mobile_shared_count` (int): Number of applicants sharing this mobile number.
7. `bank_account_shared_count` (int): Graph degree of bank account node.
8. `bank_penny_drop_status` (binary: 0 or 1): PFMS bank verification pass.
9. `bank_name_fuzzy_score` (float: 0–100): Bank account holder name vs farmer name.
10. `land_record_found` (binary: 0 or 1): Bhulekh title existence.
11. `land_area_discrepancy_ratio` (float): Declared area / Bhulekh registered area.
12. `land_owner_aadhaar_match` (binary: 0 or 1): Direct Aadhaar hash match in registry.
13. `is_agricultural_land` (binary: 0 or 1): Land use classification check.
14. `statutory_exclusion_hit` (binary: 0 or 1): Hard exclusion master match.
15. `duplicate_parcel_claim_count` (int): Number of claims on same `parcel_id`.
16. `village_density_ratio` (float): Current village registrations vs historical cultivator baseline.
17. `temporal_surge_velocity_ratio` (float): District registration rate vs 14-day rolling baseline.
18. `isolation_forest_anomaly_score` (float: -0.5 to 0.5): Raw unsupervised decision function.
19. `syndicate_cluster_size` (int): NetworkX connected component node count.
20. `engine_flags_total_count` (int): Total number of anomaly flags raised.
21. `engine_critical_flags_count` (int): Count of flags with severity Critical.
22. `engine_high_flags_count` (int): Count of flags with severity High.
23. `engine_medium_flags_count` (int): Count of flags with severity Medium.
24. `engine_low_flags_count` (int): Count of flags with severity Low.

### State Management & Bootstrap Lifecycle
- On server startup (`backend/app/main.py` lifespan event):
  - Check if pre-trained model artifact `backend/models/xgboost_risk_model.json` exists.
  - If missing, execute `train_or_load_xgboost_model()` using deterministic synthetic reference distributions generated with seed=42.
  - Initialize and cache the global `TreeExplainer` instance in memory for instant reuse.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic
```python
from pydantic import BaseModel, Field
from typing import List, Tuple, Optional

class SHAPDriver(BaseModel):
    feature_name: str = Field(description="Normalized feature key name")
    attribution_value: float = Field(description="Exact TreeSHAP attribution value")
    description: str = Field(description="Human-readable explanation of the driver")

class MLRiskEvaluationResult(BaseModel):
    rule_risk_score: int = Field(ge=0, le=100, description="Heuristic sum of flag severities")
    ml_risk_score: int = Field(ge=0, le=100, description="Calibrated XGBoost fraud probability")
    final_hybrid_score: int = Field(ge=0, le=100, description="Ensemble score enforcing statutory overrides")
    is_statutory_override: bool = Field(description="True if score was locked to 100 by legal disqualification")
    divergence_score: int = Field(description="Difference between ML score and Rule score (detects hidden interactions)")
    top_shap_drivers: List[SHAPDriver] = Field(description="Top 3 predictive drivers explaining the ML assessment")
```

### Async-First Design
CPU-bound feature matrix formatting and tree evaluation are wrapped in `anyio.to_thread.run_sync` or executed synchronously given sub-5ms latency, preventing blocking the FastAPI async event loop.

### Reproducibility & Traceability
- All training routines fix `random_state=42`.
- Each evaluated application records the model version hash and exact feature inputs in `AnomalyReport.evidence_json["ml_vector"]`.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric (Pass/Fail or Target) | Measurement Approach | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Statutory Exclusion Integrity** | 100% Pass (0 false negatives on hard exclusion test cases) | Automated test asserting `final_hybrid_score == 100` and `is_statutory_override == True` across all statutory exclusion fixtures. | Critical |
| **Syndicate Interaction Detection** | ≥ 92% Recall on multi-signal syndicate cases | Evaluated on synthetic syndicate scenarios where individual rule scores are < 50. | Critical |
| **Clean Farmer Specificity** | ≤ 4% False Positive Rate on verified genuine cultivators | Evaluated on 30 clean reference applications. | High |
| **Inference Latency** | ≤ 10ms per application (99th percentile) | Python `time.perf_counter()` benchmark during test runs. | High |
| **SHAP Local Accuracy** | 100% (Sum of SHAP values + base value == raw model prediction margin) | Mathematical identity validation across test batch. | High |
| **Model Calibration (Brier Score)** | ≤ 0.12 on synthetic ground-truth validation set | Scikit-learn `brier_score_loss` calculation. | Medium |

### Eval Tooling
- **Primary Tool**: Pytest + Scikit-Learn Metrics (`brier_score_loss`, `roc_auc_score`, `confusion_matrix`) + TreeSHAP fidelity test.
- **Test Suite Location**: `backend/tests/test_xgboost_risk.py`
- **CI/CD Integration**:
```bash
pytest backend/tests/test_xgboost_risk.py -v
```

### Reference Dataset
- **Size**: 60 deterministically generated applications with ground-truth binary labels ($y \in \{0, 1\}$).
- **Composition**:
  - 30 Clean Genuine Farmers (Single land parcel, verified bank, valid OTP, normal village density).
  - 10 Hard Statutory Exclusions (Taxpayers, deceased individuals, institutional titles).
  - 10 Non-Linear Syndicates (Shared bank accounts + moderate phonetic drift + registration surge).
  - 10 Boundary / Ambiguous Cases (Minor area discrepancies, pending land survey updates).

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| **Statutory Exclusion Override** | `statutory_exclusion_hit == 1` or `EXCLUSION_*` flag present | Force `final_hybrid_score = 100`, bypass probabilistic decay, and flag `is_statutory_override = True`. |
| **Missing Feature Sanitizer** | Any NaN, Inf, or unmapped value in the 24-dim feature vector | Automatically imputes safe defaults (`-1.0` for ratios, `0` for counts), logs diagnostic alert, and prevents pipeline crash. |
| **Score Bounding Assurance** | Output score outside `[0, 100]` | Hard clamp `min(100, max(0, score))`. |

### Offline (Model Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|---|---|---|
| **Rule-vs-ML Divergence Rate** | Continuous tracking in audit logs (`abs(ml_risk_score - rule_risk_score) > 30`) | If divergence exceeds 20% of live claims, trigger an automated officer review report to inspect feature distribution shifts. |
| **Brier Calibration Score** | Re-computed during synthetic benchmark evaluation endpoint (`/api/admin/evaluation/metrics`) | Alert if Brier score > 0.15, triggering automated tree hyperparameter recalibration. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Key Metrics to Track:**
1. `ml_inference_time_ms`: Time taken for vector extraction + tree inference + SHAP computation.
2. `divergence_surge_count`: Frequency of applications where ML flagged critical risk while rule sum was low.
3. `top_shap_feature_frequency`: Distribution of which features are most frequently driving positive risk attributions.
4. `statutory_override_frequency`: Count of applications gated by legal disqualifications.

**Smart Sampling Strategy for Officer Adjudication:**
- All applications with `divergence_score >= 35` are automatically highlighted with an orange `ML Suspicion Surge` badge in the Admin Applications Review table.
- Scheme officers can filter specifically by "ML Divergence" to investigate sophisticated syndicate claims that slipped under simple heuristic radar.
