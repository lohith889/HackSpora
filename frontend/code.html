Am I eligible for PM-KISAN if my family owns 3 acres, and what documents are required for e-KYC?

10:42 AM · Sent
KG
Verified PM-KISAN Operational Guidelines Confidence: 99.2%

Yes, you are eligible. Under current PM-KISAN rules, the previous 2-hectare land cap was removed. All landholding farmer families with cultivable land recorded in their name are eligible, subject to standard exclusion criteria.

Mandatory e-KYC Requirements:
  • Aadhaar Card: Linked to your active mobile number.
  • Land Records: Verified RoR / Khatauni in the beneficiary's name.
  • Bank Account: Aadhaar-seeded & NPCI-mapped for DBT transfers.
Important Warning: Institutional landholders, income-tax payers, and government employees remain excluded.
